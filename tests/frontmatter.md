# Subject-specific Examination Regulations (Front Matter)

*Front matter · p. i*

## Subject-specific Examination Regulations for Software, Data and Technology

*(Fachspezifische Prüfungsordnung)*

The subject-specific examination regulations for Software, Data and Technology are defined by this
program handbook and are valid only in combination with the General Examination Regulations for
Undergraduate degree programs (General Examination Regulations = Rahmenprüfungsordnung). This
handbook also contains the program-specific Study and Examination Plan (Chapter 6).

Upon graduation, students in this program will receive a Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree with a
scope of 180 ECTS (for specifics see Chapter 4 of this handbook).

## Notes

- The front matter says the handbook contains the Study and Examination Plan in "Chapter 6". In
  the table of contents (p. ii) Chapter 5 is the *Study and Examination Plan*; Chapter 6 is
  *Software, Data and Technology Modules*.
- "for specifics see Chapter 4 of this handbook" points at the degree and the 180 ECTS scope.
  Chapter 4 is the *Schematic Study Plan for Software, Data and Technology*; the degree and the
  graduation credit total are stated in Chapter 3 (§3.3 Degree, §3.4 Graduation Requirements).
- The version table has no ruling lines separating its rows in the source, and its cells are not
  aligned into one row per version. Read from the page layout, it holds three data rows: the
  current version with its validity date and no decision date, an entry with the June 28, 2023
  decision and no validity date, and an entry pairing Sep 01, 2023 with the May 24, 2023 original
  approval. No version numbers are given for the two 2023 entries.
- The heading is printed as "Subject-specific Examination Regulations for Software, Data and
  Technology"; the abbreviation SDT used throughout the rest of the handbook is not introduced
  here.
